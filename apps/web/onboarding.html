What should we call it?

This is the organisation Kalyx will govern. Your workspace is created alongside it as the tenant boundary.

Give it an executive.

Your CEO is the intelligence and orchestration layer. It can plan and delegate, but Kalyx policies still decide what may execute.

Execution stays governedThe CEO does not receive unrestricted provider credentials or direct execution authority.

Set the boundaries.

These are the organisation's hard operating limits. They apply before consequential actions are authorized.

Core Kalyx invariants remain enforcedYour policy configuration cannot disable the control-plane safety boundary.

Connect compute.

Connect the infrastructure your agents will use. Provider secrets stay behind the Kalyx provider boundary and are never handed to agents.

Governed provider connectionDelegated/OAuth connection can be used where the provider supports it. Kalyx will not ask for private wallet credentials.
